1.  components
1.1Overview

The Table of Contents (TOC) feature provides a navigation panel on the left side of the document viewer. It acts as a roadmap for the document, allowing users to quickly jump to specific sections. The TOC is dynamic, meaning it automatically updates to highlight the section currently being viewed, ensuring users always know their location within the document.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Document Parsing: The system scans the document sections and identifies their structure.
2.Hierarchy Formatting: The system checks section titles for specific numbering patterns (e.g., "3.1"). If a pattern is found, the item is treated as a sub-section and indented in the list; otherwise, it is treated as a main section.
3.Display: The TOC is rendered in the left panel, showing the list of sections with their corresponding numbers and titles.
4.Navigation: When a user clicks a section title in the TOC, the main document view smoothly scrolls to that specific section.
5.Active State Tracking: As the user scrolls through the document, the system monitors which section is currently visible on the screen and automatically highlights that corresponding item in the TOC.
1.3Key Concepts
–Dynamic Hierarchy: The TOC automatically distinguishes between main sections and sub-sections based on the title format, creating a clean, indented visual structure.
–Active State: A visual indicator that keeps the TOC in sync with the user's current position in the document, providing helpful context during long reads.
–Smooth Navigation: A "click-to-scroll" interaction that allows users to bypass manual scrolling and jump directly to the content they need.
1.4Business Value

This feature significantly improves the user experience by reducing the time spent searching for information within long documents. By providing a clear, interactive navigation aid, users can maintain focus and efficiency, ensuring they can easily reference or jump between critical sections of the document without losing their place.

2.  LOGIC
2.1Overview

The Table of Contents (TOC) feature provides a navigation panel on the left side of your document workspace. It acts as a dynamic outline, allowing you to view, organize, and quickly jump to specific parts of your document. The system automatically keeps this list updated as you add new sections or subsections, ensuring you always have a clear view of your document's structure.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Loading the Document: When you open a document, the system automatically retrieves all existing sections and displays them in the left-hand panel, ordered by their position in the document.
2.Adding Content: You can click the "Add Section" or "Add Subsection" buttons to create new parts of your document.
3.Automatic Updates: Once a new section is created, the system instantly adds it to your Table of Contents list.
4.Navigation: The system automatically selects the newly created section and scrolls your main view directly to that area, so you can begin editing immediately.
5.Visual Cues: If a section was generated or assisted by AI, a small badge will appear next to its title in the Table of Contents, helping you identify AI-authored content at a glance.
2.3Key Concepts
–Section: A distinct block of content within your document that appears as an entry in the Table of Contents.
–Table of Contents (TOC): The sidebar navigation panel that lists all document sections in their correct order.
–Active Section: The specific part of the document you are currently viewing or editing, highlighted in the sidebar for easy reference.
–AI Label: A visual indicator (badge) that marks content created by the AI, providing transparency regarding the source of the text.
2.4Business Value
–Improved Navigation: Users can jump to specific document areas instantly, saving time spent scrolling through long documents.
–Enhanced Organization: By providing a clear, hierarchical view of sections and subsections, the feature helps users maintain a logical structure throughout the document creation process.
–Transparency: The AI badge ensures users are always aware of which parts of the document were generated by AI, supporting better content management and review workflows.
–Seamless Workflow: The automatic scrolling and selection features reduce manual effort, allowing users to focus on writing rather than managing the document interface.
3.  DATA
3.1Overview

The Table of Contents (TOC) feature provides a dynamic navigation panel on the left side of the document editor. It allows users to view the structure of their document, including all sections and subsections, in the correct order. This feature ensures that as users add or organize content, the navigation panel stays synchronized, making it easy to jump to specific parts of the document.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Load: When a document is opened, the system automatically retrieves the full list of sections associated with that document.
2.Ordering: The system organizes these sections based on their assigned position, ensuring the Table of Contents matches the document's layout.
3.Display: The retrieved list is rendered in the left-hand panel, providing a clear overview of the document structure.
4.Dynamic Updates: When a user adds a new section or subsection, the system calculates its correct position in the hierarchy and saves it.
5.Refresh: Immediately after a new section is created, the Table of Contents updates to include the new entry, ensuring the navigation panel is always current.
3.3Key Concepts
–Section: A distinct block of content within a document that can be tracked and navigated to via the Table of Contents.
–Hierarchy: The relationship between sections and subsections, which determines how the document is structured and displayed in the navigation panel.
–Positioning: A system-managed order that ensures sections appear in the Table of Contents exactly as they are intended to appear in the document.
–Synchronization: The process of automatically updating the Table of Contents whenever the document structure changes, such as adding a new section.
3.4Business Value
–Improved Navigation: Users can quickly jump to specific sections of long or complex documents, saving time and reducing manual scrolling.
–Enhanced Document Clarity: By providing a visual outline, the Table of Contents helps users maintain a clear understanding of the document's structure and flow.
–Seamless Editing Experience: Because the Table of Contents updates automatically when new sections are added, users can focus on content creation without needing to manually manage or refresh their navigation tools.
