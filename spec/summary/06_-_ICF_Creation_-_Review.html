1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The ICF Review page is the final stage in the document creation process. It provides a centralized workspace where reviewers can examine the draft document, track its history, and provide feedback. The interface is designed to be intuitive, separating the document content from the administrative tools needed to finalize the ICF.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Document Loading: Upon entering the page, the system retrieves the full document content and the complete history of actions taken on that document.
2.Document Visualization: The document is displayed in the center of the screen, organized into distinct sections. Each section is presented as a card, clearly identifying whether the content was generated by AI.
3.Navigation: A table of contents on the left allows users to jump quickly to specific sections of the document.
4.Feedback Loop: Reviewers use the "Comments" tab on the right panel to provide specific feedback or suggestions for changes.
5.History Tracking: Reviewers can switch to the "Audit Trail" tab to view a chronological record of all activities, including who made edits, when comments were posted, and the overall progression of the document.
6.Finalization: Once the review is complete, the reviewer uses the bottom navigation bar to move the document forward in the workflow.
1.3Key Concepts
–Section Cards: A modular way of displaying document content that highlights AI-generated text, making it easy for human reviewers to identify areas that may require closer scrutiny.
–Audit Trail: A transparent, time-stamped log of every significant action taken on the document, ensuring full accountability and traceability for compliance purposes.
–Comment Panel: A dedicated space for collaborative feedback, allowing reviewers to communicate directly within the context of the document.
–Progress Stepper: A visual indicator at the top of the page that shows the current stage of the document within the broader creation workflow.
1.4Business Value

This feature ensures high-quality, compliant ICF documents by facilitating a structured review process. By providing clear visibility into AI-generated content and maintaining a comprehensive audit trail, the system reduces the risk of errors and ensures that all feedback is captured and addressed. This transparency supports regulatory compliance and streamlines the transition from draft to final document.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The ICF Review feature provides a dedicated workspace for stakeholders to examine draft Informed Consent Forms (ICFs). This interface allows reviewers to view the current content of a document alongside a comprehensive history of all actions taken on that document. By combining the document’s current state with a clear audit trail, the system ensures that reviewers have the necessary context to provide feedback and track the document's progress through the approval lifecycle.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Document Loading: When a user opens the Review Page, the system automatically retrieves the full document details, including its current content, status, and version information.
2.Content Display: The system renders the document in the center panel, organizing the information into clear sections so the reviewer can easily read and evaluate the draft.
3.Audit Trail Retrieval: Simultaneously, the system fetches the document’s activity history, which tracks every significant action taken on the file.
4.History Presentation: The audit information is displayed in a chronological feed, showing the most recent actions at the top. This allows the reviewer to see who performed specific actions and when they occurred.
2.3Key Concepts
–Document Metadata: The essential details of the ICF, such as its name, version, current status, and timestamps, which help identify the specific draft being reviewed.
–Audit Trail: A chronological record of all interactions with the document, providing transparency and accountability by logging who performed an action and when.
–Review Interface: The visual layout of the page that separates the document content from its history, allowing for a focused review process.
2.4Business Value
–Improved Accuracy: By providing a clear view of the document and its history, reviewers can make more informed decisions when providing feedback.
–Compliance and Traceability: The audit trail ensures that every change or review action is documented, which is critical for maintaining regulatory standards and internal accountability.
–Efficiency: Centralizing the document content and its history in one view reduces the time spent searching for context, allowing for a faster and more streamlined review cycle.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

The ICF Review feature allows users to access a draft version of an Informed Consent Form (ICF) to evaluate its content and address feedback. This functionality provides a centralized view where a reviewer can see the current state of the document alongside a complete history of actions taken on it, ensuring transparency and accountability throughout the review process.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Document Retrieval: When a user opens the review screen, the system fetches the full details of the selected ICF, including its current status, content, and version information.
2.Displaying Content: The system populates the main workspace with the document’s text and metadata, allowing the reviewer to read the draft and identify areas requiring updates.
3.Audit Trail Loading: Simultaneously, the system retrieves a chronological log of all previous activities related to that specific document.
4.Review and Feedback: The reviewer examines the document and the audit history (which shows who made changes and when) to understand the context of the current draft and incorporate necessary feedback.
3.3Key Concepts
–Document State: The complete set of information regarding an ICF, including its current version, status, and the actual text content being reviewed.
–Audit Trail: A chronological record of all actions performed on a document, providing a clear timeline of edits and reviews to help users track the document's evolution.
–Metadata: Supporting information attached to the document, such as creation dates and version identifiers, which helps reviewers verify they are looking at the correct draft.
3.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the quality control process for Informed Consent Forms. By providing a clear view of the document content alongside a transparent history of changes, it reduces the risk of errors, ensures that reviewer feedback is properly addressed, and maintains a reliable record of the document's lifecycle for compliance and audit purposes.
